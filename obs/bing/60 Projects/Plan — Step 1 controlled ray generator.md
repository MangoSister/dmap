# Plan — Step 1 controlled ray generator

> [!abstract] D2 decision
> D2 constructs and audits the frozen 256-ray ordinary suite for every decision map/shell case. It does **not** traverse the hierarchy, solve intersections, tune a method threshold, or report performance. Its output is an immutable ray-input manifest plus a visual sample sheet. Only after D2 passes may these rays be connected to the exhaustive oracle and the four Step 1 methods.

## 1. Questions and scope

D2 answers four narrow questions before query code can affect the corpus:

1. Are all six ray families deterministic, numerically valid, and represented in the declared counts?
2. Do aimed and grazing rays have the intended incidence against the exact piecewise-affine-height shell surface used by the oracle?
3. Do boundary and range rays actually exercise the intended floating-point ownership events?
4. Can the complete 15-map × 3-shell × 256-ray input corpus be regenerated byte-for-byte from a small manifest?

D2 deliberately excludes oracle classifications. In particular, `F01-free` may hit or miss, an aimed ray may have an earlier hit, and `F05-near-miss` is not labeled hit/miss until the exhaustive oracle runs. This prevents the generator from selecting rays based on favorable traversal outcomes.

## 2. Reused surface contract

Let the proxy triangle have positions $P_i$, directors $N_i$, and texture coordinates $T_i$. Texture coordinates determine affine barycentrics

$$
\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\end{bmatrix}
= [T_1-T_0\;T_2-T_0]^{-1}([u,v]^T-T_0).
$$

The shell is $F(u,v,h)=P(u,v)+hN(u,v)$, where both $P$ and $N$ are affine in $(u,v)$. Inside one fixed-diagonal texture microtriangle the exact represented height is

$$h(u,v)=c+g_u u+g_v v.$$

The represented displaced surface and its tangents are

$$X(u,v)=P(u,v)+h(u,v)N(u,v),$$

$$X_u=P_u+g_uN+hN_u,\qquad X_v=P_v+g_vN+hN_v.$$

The construction normal is $n=\operatorname{normalize}(X_u\times X_v)$. At a shared height edge, D2 records every valid microtriangle owner and uses the lexicographically smallest `(cell_y, cell_x, local_index)` only to choose a reproducible construction normal. The later oracle retains boundary-equivalent ownership.

No Catmull–Clark surface and no planar world microtriangle is introduced by this generator.

## 3. Shell registry

The proxy positions and texture coordinates remain

```text
P = [(0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,1,0)]
T = [(0,0), (1,0.12), (0.16,1)]
```

Let

```text
N0 = (-0.04,-0.02,0.30)
N1 = ( 0.10,-0.03,0.26)
N2 = (-0.03, 0.11,0.27)
Nbar = (N0+N1+N2)/3
```

Then `S00-parallel` uses `Nbar` at every vertex, `S01-twisted` uses the three original directors, and `S02-stress` uses `Nbar + 3*(Ni-Nbar)`. D2 uses thickness scale one. The thickness sweep is a later scientific configuration, not part of this manifest.

The shell AABB is the coordinate-wise range of the six points $P_i+hN_i$ for $h\in\{h_{min},h_{max}\}$. This is exact for the complete triangle × height interval because a coordinate is affine over the triangle at fixed $h$, and affine in $h$ at each triangle vertex. Let $L$ be 1.5 times its diagonal unless a family defines a range-boundary distance explicitly.

## 4. Deterministic sampling primitives

- Use explicit base-2/base-3 radical inverses and Hammersley indices; do not depend on SciPy.
- Any randomized rotation comes from `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(ray_seed))` and is recorded.
- Interior target barycentrics use the square-root triangle transform followed by a frozen 0.04 barycentric inset.
- Every direction is normalized in float64 and its norm is checked against one.
- Azimuth is stratified and rotated by a seed-derived constant; it is not chosen after an oracle result.
- Logical ray IDs hash the case ID, family, subfamily, and family-local index. They do not hash measured results.

## 5. Frozen ordinary families

The family order below is the persisted sequence order and totals exactly 256 rays.

### 5.1 `F00-aimed` — 64 rays

Use 16 inset Hammersley surface targets. At every target construct rays at incidence angles $0°,25°,50°,70°$, for 64 target/angle pairs. With tangent frame $(t_1,t_2)$ and stratified azimuth $\phi$,

$$
d=-\cos\theta\,n+\sin\theta(\cos\phi\,t_1+\sin\phi\,t_2),
\qquad O=X-Ld.
$$

Set `t_min=0`, `t_max=2L`. The declared target occurs at $t=L$, but the later exhaustive oracle is allowed to find an earlier intersection.

### 5.2 `F01-free` — 64 rays

Place origins with stratified face ownership on the boundary of the shell AABB expanded by 1.5 about its center. Choose independent low-discrepancy targets in the AABB expanded by 1.2. Direct the ray from origin to target; use `t_min=0` and `t_max=4` times the unexpanded shell-AABB diagonal. This is the only ordinary family with no surface target and no generated surface incidence.

### 5.3 `F02-grazing` — 48 rays

Use 16 inset Hammersley surface targets and incidence angles $78°,84°,88°$, with the same construction as `F00`. Set `t_min=0`, `t_max=2L`. These rays test long shell-space arcs and certification tails; they are not pooled with non-grazing fallback rates.

### 5.4 `F03-boundary` — 48 rays

Build 16 deterministic landmarks: four interior grid-line points, four interior grid corners, four fixed `p00–p11` microtriangle-diagonal points, and four proxy-edge points. For each landmark emit:

1. the exact float64 landmark;
2. the adjacent representable UV point on the declared negative side; and
3. the adjacent representable UV point on the positive side.

The perturbation uses `numpy.nextafter` componentwise in a recorded local boundary-normal direction. Aim at the resulting represented point with $35°$ incidence, `t_min=0`, and `t_max=2L`. Records store event type, exact landmark, side, perturbed UV, ULP delta, and all height owners.

For a proxy-edge triplet, the exterior one-ULP point is outside the represented domain and therefore cannot honestly be called a surface target. Construct it from the canonical edge owner's affine height continuation, mark `surface_target=false`, and use it only to define the neighboring ray line. The exact and interior members remain represented-surface targets. The later oracle—not the generator—classifies the exterior probe.

### 5.5 `F04-origin-range` — 16 rays

Use four inset target points. Each produces four subfamilies:

- `surface-origin`: ray begins at the represented target, so the constructed root is at `t=0`;
- `inside-shell-origin`: origin is at the same shell $(a,b)$ with height moved by 10% of the global height range toward its interior, directed to the represented target;
- `near-t-min`: target distance is $L$ and `t_min` is one `nextafter` step below or above $L$, alternating included/excluded intent by target index;
- `near-t-max`: target distance is $L$ and `t_max` is one `nextafter` step above or below $L$, alternating included/excluded intent.

The construction metadata records the intended range relation. The later oracle determines whether other roots also exist.

### 5.6 `F05-near-miss` — 16 rays

Construct eight grazing base rays from deterministic points near the three proxy edges. For each base ray, emit a `−` and `+` parallel offset by $\delta n$ in origin space, where $\delta$ is a frozen multiple of `shell_AABB_diagonal / cells`. Pair identity, sign, and $\delta$ are recorded. D2 makes no hit/miss promise; the exhaustive oracle later classifies each member of the pair.

## 6. Input records and immutable outputs

Use a generator-only schema version `step1-ray-input-v1` so incomplete scientific `ray` records cannot be mistaken for oracle/traversal output.

Each record contains:

```text
schema_version, record_type, suite_id, configuration_hash,
case_key, grid_id, map_id, shell_id, ray_seed,
ray_id, sequence_index, family, subfamily, family_local_index,
origin_xyz, direction_xyz, t_min, t_max,
generated_incidence_degrees, target_uv, target_xyz, target_t,
target_owner_classes, construction_parameters
```

Fields not applicable to a family are `null`, not fabricated values. The run directory is

```text
experiments/step1_ray_inputs/<suite_id>/
  configuration.json
  cases.jsonl.gz
  rays.jsonl.gz
  result.json
```

`result.json` records configuration and stream hashes, record counts by family and shell, all dataset grid hashes, Python/NumPy versions, and figure paths. Existing complete suites are immutable; a changed visualization or construction version creates a new suite ID.

## 7. Sample-sheet figure

Before traversal, export PDF/SVG/PNG under `figures/step1_rays/`. Use one frozen representative case, `P03-sine-bump × S02-stress`, with six separate panels—one per family. Show the proxy triangular prism/shell envelope, a sparse fixed subset of rays, construction targets when present, a zoomed exact/negative/positive triplet for `F03`, and a zoomed paired offset for `F05`.

The caption must state that the figure audits input construction only; it contains no traversal or performance result.

## 8. Tests before oracle/traversal integration

1. The shell registry contains exactly S00/S01/S02 and matches the frozen director formulas bit-for-bit.
2. Every decision case has counts `64/64/48/48/16/16`, total 256, with stable sequence and unique ray IDs.
3. Repeated generation gives byte-identical canonical records and stream hashes.
4. Every ray is finite, has unit direction within `8 eps`, and satisfies `t_min <= t_max`.
5. Every targeted ray contains its recorded target on the world ray within `128 eps` times the shell diagonal.
6. `F00/F02/F03` generated incidence agrees with the constructed surface normal within `2e-11` degrees away from declared boundary ambiguity.
7. Every target UV lies in the proxy triangle and its stored height/world point matches the chosen affine microtriangle within the declared float tolerance.
8. `F01` origins lie on the recorded expanded-AABB face and its targets lie inside the 1.2-expanded AABB.
9. Every `F03` triplet has one exact point and strict, adjacent representable values on opposite recorded sides; all declared event classes occur four times.
10. `F04` constructed target parameters have the recorded on/below/above range relation, independent of later closest-hit classification.
11. Every `F05` pair has identical direction and origins separated by exactly `2 delta n` within roundoff.
12. The full 45-case manifest contains 11,520 rays, all 15 D1 grid IDs, all three shells, and reopens with matching hashes.
13. The sample sheet contains all six family labels and exports nonempty PDF/SVG/PNG files.
14. All existing 50 P1–D1 tests remain unchanged and pass.

**Gate D2:** all fourteen checks pass and the sample sheet is visually audited. Only then integrate the frozen inputs into a new lifecycle configuration and run the exhaustive oracle before enabling hierarchy variants.

## 9. Implementation sequence

1. Add the pure shell registry, exact represented-surface evaluator, and low-discrepancy helpers.
2. Implement and unit-test one family at a time in the frozen order.
3. Add canonical generator-only records and immutable manifest emission.
4. Generate and inspect the sample sheet; a figure-only change increments its version and preserves prior artifacts.
5. Generate the complete 45-case input manifest and independently reopen/hash/count it.
6. Record D2 completion here and in the parent Step 1/project plans.

No traversal predicate, curve certificate, root solver, or CUDA code changes during D2.
